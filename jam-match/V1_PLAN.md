# Jam Match: version 1 plan

**Goal:** prove that musicians in one city will actually meet and jam through the app.
v1 is **Duo mode only**. Band mode comes once duos work.

## Must-have

1. **Sign up & account**
   - Email, Google or Apple login.
   - Date of birth; block anyone under 18. Show age only, never the birthday.
   - City and location. Show approximate distance only, never an address.
   - Profile photo.
2. **Profile**
   - Instruments, with a level for each (Beginner, Casual, Intermediate, Advanced, Pro).
   - Genres, short bio, optional link to a clip (YouTube, Instagram, SoundCloud).
   - **Favourite musicians: top 5.** Connect Apple Music to fill them in automatically,
     or type them. Shown on the profile, with the ones you share highlighted.
3. **Preferences:** distance, age range, level range, genre openness, instruments wanted.
4. **Duo mode:** swipe cards of people who pass both sides' filters. A mutual "Let's jam" is a match.
5. **Chat**
   - Text chat between matches, with 3 suggested openers to break the ice, for example:
     - "Cool, you wanna jam?"
     - "I see you play drums. How long have you been playing?"
     - "What's a song you've been wanting to play?"
   - Notifications for new matches and messages.
6. **Safety:** block and report, 18+ check, privacy policy.
7. **Keep it alive:** hide profiles after 30 days of inactivity, with a "Show me again" button.

## Later (v2+)

- Band mode (groups of 3–10), which can run alongside Duo mode like Bumble's modes.
- Plan-a-jam card: time, place (home, practice room, park, uni), cost split, voting.
- "Learn from older, experienced players" opt-in.
- Learn tab (drums → chords → bass → melody). A page of YouTube links could ship early.
- Spotify connect: Spotify limits unapproved apps to a handful of test users
  and only approves big, registered businesses, so it waits.
- Audio uploads, "would play again" ratings, real ID verification, studio booking.

## Build order (about 10 hours a week)

| Step | What | Rough time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Setup: run the app on your phone | 1–2 weeks (including learning) |
| 2 | Sign up + profile | 2–3 weeks |
| 3 | Preferences + matching logic | 2 weeks |
| 4 | Swipe + match | 2 weeks |
| 5 | Chat, openers, notifications | 2 weeks |
| 6 | Safety, polish, test with 5–10 musician friends | 2 weeks |

## How we'll know v1 works

Set targets before launch, for example: 50 musicians signed up in your city
and 10 jams that really happened. Ask everyone who jammed: "Would you use this again?"
